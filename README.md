## Course overview

CS 485/585: Computer Networks is a theoretical and practical study of
computer networks: how they're structured, how protocols work together, how
data finds its way from one place to another reliably and efficiently.
Alongside the technical material, the course looks at where networks
physically operate and who they do or don't serve, grounded in
New Mexico's broadband landscape.

This page, including the reading list, calendar, and policies, is under
active development ahead of the first class on Tuesday, August 18, 2026.
