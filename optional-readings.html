Optional Readings

CS 485/585 • Fall 2026 • University of New Mexico

< back to syllabus

These are optional readings and resources for diving deeper into course topics (in no particular order). Graduate students may also find these useful as a starting point for exploring ideas for their final project.

This is by no means an exhaustive list, just projects or research areas I am aware of. If you want to look beyond this list, check out the proceedings from these networking conferences and workshops:

  • ACM SIGCOMM (networking research broadly)
  • USENIX NSDI (networked and distributed systems)
  • ACM MobiCom (mobile and wireless networking)
  • ACM IMC (Internet measurement)
  • TPRC (communications, information, and Internet policy)

Internet Architecture and Infrastructure

  • "The Design Philosophy of the DARPA Internet Protocols." David D. Clark. ACM SIGCOMM, 1988.
  • "Rethinking the Design of the Internet: The End-to-End Arguments vs. the Brave New World." Marjory S. Blumenthal & David D. Clark. ACM Transactions on Internet Technology, 2001.
  • "Steps Toward an Ecology of Infrastructure: Design and Access for Large Information Spaces." Susan Leigh Star & Karen Ruhleder. Information Systems Research, 1996.
  • "Understanding Infrastructure: Dynamics, Tensions, and Design." Paul N. Edwards, Steven J. Jackson, Geoffrey C. Bowker & Cory P. Knobel. NSF Workshop Report, 2007.

Community-Run Networks

  • NYC Mesh
  • Seattle Community Network
  • Güifi.net, probably the most successful example of a community network. Lots of info online, but here is one article.
  • SwampNet

Indigenous Knowledge and Communities

  • Network Sovereignty: Building the Internet across Indian Country. Marisa Elena Duarte. University of Washington Press, 2017.
  • "Toward Indigenous Presence: A Radical Relationality Approach for Designing Mixed-Reality Indigenous Data Experiences." Sean J. Dorr, James W. Rock, Vicente M. Diaz & Daniel F. Keefe. IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications, 2024.
  • Tribal Broadband Bootcamp
  • "Decolonizing Dynamic Spectrum Sharing." Darrah Blackwater, Ilia Murtazashvili & Kevin Gifford. TPRC, 2024.

Alternative Technologies

  • "LoRaX: Repurposing LoRa as a Low Data Rate Messaging System to Extend Internet Boundaries." Morgan Vigil-Hayes, Md Nazmul Hossain, Alexander K. Elliott, Elizabeth M. Belding & Ellen Zegura. ACM COMPASS, 2022.
  • "El Paquete Semanal: The Week's Internet in Havana." Michaelanne Dye, David Nemer, Josiah Mangiameli, Amy S. Bruckman & Neha Kumar. ACM CHI, 2018.
  • "Post-grid Imaginaries: Electricity, Generators, and the Future of Energy." Joanne Randa Nucho. Public Culture, 2022.
  • "A Delay-Tolerant Network Architecture for Challenged Internets." Kevin Fall. ACM SIGCOMM, 2003.
  • "SaTE: Low-Latency Traffic Engineering for Satellite Networks." Hao Wu, Yizhan Han, Mohit Rajpal, Qizhen Zhang & Jingxian Wang. ACM SIGCOMM, 2025.

Policy and Censorship

  • "A Multi-Perspective View of Internet Censorship in Myanmar." Ramakrishna Padmanabhan, Arturo Filastò, Maria Xynou, Ram Sundara Raman, Kennedy Middleton, Mingwei Zhang, Doug Madory, Molly Roberts & Alberto Dainotti. ACM SIGCOMM FOCI Workshop, 2021.
  • "Network Neutrality, Broadband Discrimination." Tim Wu. Journal of Telecommunications and High Technology Law, 2003.

Emergency Response Networks

  • bitchat by AOS
  • "Crisis Informatics: New Data for Extraordinary Times." Leysia Palen & Kenneth M. Anderson. Science, 2016.

Rural Context

  • "Technology on the Trail." D. Scott McCrickard, Michael A. Horning, Steve Harrison, Ellie Harmon, Alan Dix, Norman Makoto Su & Timothy Stelter. ACM GROUP, 2018.
  • "Rural HCI Research: Definitions, Distinctions, Methods, and Opportunities." Jean Hardy, Susan Wyche & Tiffany Veinot. ACM CSCW, 2019.

Network Measurements (Performance and Beyond)

  • "Analyzing Disparity and Temporal Progression of Internet Quality through Crowdsourced Measurements with Bias-Correction." Hyeongseong Lee, Udit Paul, Arpit Gupta, Elizabeth Belding & Mengyang Gu. arXiv preprint, 2023.
  • IODA (Internet Outage Detection and Analysis)
  • CellWatch

Remote Sensing

  • "Battery-less Zero-maintenance Embedded Sensing at the Mithræum of Circus Maximus." Mikhail Afanasov, Naveed Anwar Bhatti, Dennis Campagna, Giacomo Caslini, Fabio Massimo Centonze, Koustabh Dolui, Andrea Maioli, Erica Barone, Muhammad Hamad Alizai, Junaid Haroon Siddiqui & Luca Mottola. ACM SenSys, 2020.
  • "A Workflow for Microclimate Sensor Networks: Integrating Geographic Tools, Statistics, and Local Knowledge." David H. Klinges, Jonas J. Lembrechts, Stijn Van de Vondel, Eric J. Greenlee, Kian Hayles-Cotton & Rebecca A. Senior. Ecological Informatics, 2025.
